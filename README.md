## What this is

A one-page newspaper front page that sums up the world news of Sunday
22 November 2026 in a single 1024 × 1024 image. The masthead names the
paper — THE WORLD TODAY — with the date SUNDAY 22 NOVEMBER 2026 large and
unmissable. The hero story is the Baltic Sea on fire: drones hit the
Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland, the ship burned and
drifted into Finnish waters, its 16 crew took to lifeboats, and Finland
tightened security as the war spills into NATO waters. Below it, three
panels cover the COP31 climate talks deadlocked in overtime with no deal
on fossil fuels, tankers hit off Oman as US–Iran tensions rise, and the
Pentagon curbing US–Canada military contacts. A bottom ticker labelled
ALSO carries five more stories: the magnitude 6.9 earthquake in Peru's
Andes, Jair Bolsonaro in intensive care in Brasilia, Australia ending
England's seven-match T20 winning streak, AI possibly helping solve a
famous maths problem, and Cyclone Akvan cancelling flights in India.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes `entry.png` into `out/` (or into the directory named by the
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable). The program uses only the
Python standard library, reads no files, makes no network requests, and
is deterministic — repeated runs produce byte-identical output.

The image is drawn entirely from code: flat shapes for the sea, the
burning ship, the coast-guard vessel and the drones, plus a hand-built
5×7 bitmap font for every letter.
